On keeper "worst values": every locked keeper (Judkins, Mahomes, Skattebo, Dowdle, Waddle) shows up as a below-market price on its own team's card. That's expected, not a mistake — a keeper price is last year's bargain plus the league's standard +$10 tax, so it will almost never look like a "value" pick against a fresh open-market price.
